One status field can bury a critical alert

ServiceNow scores every alert like an odometer. The leading digits come from the business services the alert affects. A service instance counts only when its Operational Status is Operational. If it isn't, those leading digits read zero, whatever the outage looks like.

Where the points come from

Points shown for the operational row. A struck-through line is what the second row loses.

Formula, from ServiceNow's alert priority documentation: services × 1,000,000 + severity × 100,000 + CI type × 100 + role × 10 + secondary count + state × 0.001. The Alerts list shows the score divided by 1,000.

Services value is the sum of each impacted service's mapped business criticality (1 - most critical = 4 points down to 4 - not critical = 1). Only Service Instance classes (cmdb_ci_service_auto and extensions) that are operational take part in impact calculation.

Severity, role, and CI type point values here are illustrative. Your instance's values live in em_alert_priority_category_mapping and em_alert_priority_ci_type.
